Use case: stylized-concept. Create a production panoramic banner for SlotAvailability's search page, matching the reference's cinematic hand-painted ADVENTURE illustration, warm amber sunlight and deep plum shadows. Reference is style inspiration, not an image to duplicate. Wide panoramic 3:1 composition, artwork fills entire frame edge to edge, NO text, NO logo, NO typography, NO UI, NO frame. Central focal point an elegant old brass magnifying glass resting diagonally over a curling parchment exploration map, revealing a tiny luminous violet gemstone through the lens, with a brass compass alongside. Across the background a continuous panoramic fictional adventure landscape: Egyptian temple and palms on left, distant warm city at center, alpine mountain and tropical waterfall at right. A subtle glowing amber dotted exploration route connects distant locations. Original game-world art, mature richly painted, inviting discovery and searching, not shiny 3D product objects, not stock-photo ecommerce, no coins or slot machine. Strong bold shapes, well-lit readable central magnifying glass, controlled detail for thin banner. Dark plum atmospheric edges blend into site #151622.
